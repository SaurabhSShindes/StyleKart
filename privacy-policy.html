Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to StyleKart!

Welcome to StyleKart. This is a demonstration e-commerce website created to showcase shopping and account features. We respect your privacy and explain below how information is handled on this demo website.

Information We Collect

Our demo website may store your name, email address and shopping cart details in your browser's Local Storage.

How We Use Information

This information is used to demonstrate login, account management and shopping cart features.

Data Security

Local Storage is not encrypted and should not be considered secure. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

Cookies & Local Storage

StyleKart uses browser Local Storage for basic website functionality. Data remains in the browser where it was saved.

Your Privacy

  • Your demo data is stored locally in your browser.
  • Clearing browser storage may remove saved website data.
  • Data saved on one device does not automatically sync to another device.
  • Avoid using real or reused passwords on this demo website.
